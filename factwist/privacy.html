Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy describes how Factwist Uploader (the "App") handles data. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the Factwist YouTube channels and is used only by that owner.

What the App accesses

With the owner's permission granted through Google OAuth, the App uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set video titles, descriptions, tags and publishing schedules, and manage playlists on the owner's own Factwist channels. It also reads public view and like counts of the owner's own videos to build a private daily statistics report for the owner.

What data is stored

The App stores OAuth tokens on the owner's computer and in the owner's private Cloudflare account so it can upload videos without signing in each time. The App does not collect, store or process data about any other YouTube users or viewers.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking access in your Google account settings disables the App's tokens. Locally stored tokens are deleted by the owner on request or when the App is no longer used.
