Loot Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Loot Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the LOOT channels to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.

What data it uses

Through the YouTube Data API the tool uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish time, and adds them to playlists on channels the owner has authorized. It reads basic channel information (name and ID) to choose the right channel.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private automation environment. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, and use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
